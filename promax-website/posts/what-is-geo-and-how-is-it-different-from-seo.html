SEO & GEO

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

By Pro Max Solution Marketing Agency 14 Sep 2026 5 min read

SEO helps your pages rank in a list of search results. GEO, short for generative engine optimisation, helps your brand get mentioned and cited inside answers written by AI tools such as Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity. You need both, and most of the work overlaps.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

With classic SEO, the goal is a high position in a list of links. A person searches, scans the results and clicks one. With GEO, the goal is to be part of the answer itself. The AI tool reads many sources, writes a short response and often links to or names the sources it used. Your content needs to be easy to find, easy to trust and easy to quote.

SEOGEO
GoalRank pages in search resultsBe cited or recommended in AI-written answers
Where it shows upSearch results pagesAI Overviews, chat assistants, answer engines
What you measureRankings, clicks, organic leadsMentions, citations, referral traffic from AI tools, branded searches
Content styleTargets keywords and search intentAnswers whole questions clearly, with facts worth quoting

What stays the same

The basics still matter, because AI tools draw on the same open web that search engines crawl. Fast, crawlable pages, a clear site structure, genuinely helpful content and a good reputation help both SEO and GEO. If your SEO foundations are weak, fix them first.

What changes with GEO

  • Answer first. Put the direct answer in the first two sentences, then explain it.
  • Write for questions. Use headings that match how people actually ask, such as "How much does a website cost?"
  • Be specific. Numbers, examples, dates and named sources give AI tools something concrete to quote. Vague marketing language does not.
  • Keep your facts consistent. Your business name, services and contact details should match across your website, Google Business Profile and social profiles.
  • Earn mentions elsewhere. Reviews, directories, partner websites and press coverage help AI tools trust that your business exists and is credible.
  • Add structured data. Organization, Article and FAQ markup helps machines understand your pages. It supports visibility but does not guarantee it.

How to start this month

  1. List 20 questions your ideal customers ask before they buy.
  2. Ask those questions in the AI tools your customers use, and note which sources get cited.
  3. Rewrite your five most important pages so each one answers its main question in the first two sentences.
  4. Publish helpful articles on a steady schedule, one clear question per article.
  5. Repeat the check every month and track what changes.

A word of caution

Nobody can guarantee that an AI tool will mention your business. These systems change often and do not publish their ranking rules. Be wary of any agency that promises a guaranteed spot in AI answers. Look for one that shows you a plan and reports on what it can actually measure.

Frequently asked questions

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO. The same foundations, such as helpful content, fast pages and a good reputation, support both. GEO adds a focus on clear answers and consistent facts that AI tools can quote.

Is GEO the same as AEO?

They overlap. AEO (answer engine optimisation) usually refers to winning direct answers such as featured snippets and voice results. GEO focuses on being cited in answers written by generative AI tools. The tactics are largely the same.

How long does GEO take to work?

It depends on your starting point and competition. SEO improvements usually take months, not days, and AI answers change often, so plan for steady work and monthly checks rather than a one-off fix.
