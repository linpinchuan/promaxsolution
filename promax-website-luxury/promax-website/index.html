Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you get in touch.

What services does Pro Max Solution offer?

We run Facebook Ads and Google Ads, provide SEO and GEO, and develop websites, apps, business systems and AI tools.

Do I need a large advertising budget to start?

No. We recommend starting with one channel and a budget large enough to collect useful data, then scaling what works. The right amount depends on your industry and goals.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO usually takes months rather than days. Technical fixes can show sooner, while content and authority build over time. We report progress every month so you can see what is changing.

Can you build my website and run my marketing?

Yes. We handle both, so your website is built with tracking, speed and lead capture in mind from the start.

What is GEO and do I need it?

GEO (generative engine optimisation) helps your business get mentioned and cited in answers from AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews. It builds on SEO and is worth adding if your customers use AI assistants to research what to buy.

How will I know if it is working?

You receive a monthly report in plain language showing leads, cost per lead and the actions we took, plus what we plan to change next.
